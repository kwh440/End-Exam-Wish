# Build a Premium Interactive Exam-Wish Website

Create a stunning, premium-quality, single-page website that wishes a girl good luck for her upcoming exam.

The experience should feel cinematic, emotional, luxurious, and memorable rather than childish or overly romantic. The design should resemble a premium digital greeting card mixed with an Apple-style product page and modern storytelling website.

## Tech Requirements

* HTML5
* CSS3
* Vanilla JavaScript
* GSAP (ScrollTrigger, Text animations)
* Responsive Design
* No frameworks
* Clean and well-commented code
* Smooth scrolling
* High performance

### Section 3 – Poem Experience

This is the most important section.

Use:

background.JFIF

as the background image.

Requirements:

* Fullscreen section
* Dark overlay for readability
* Background fixed with parallax effect
* Elegant blur overlay
* Text always highly visible

Typography:

* Font: Playfair Display for poem
* Fallback: Georgia, serif
* Font color: Gold (#FFD700)
* Subtle text shadow
* Desktop font size: 2rem
* Mobile font size: 1.1rem
* Line height: 2

Poem must appear EXACTLY as written below.

DO NOT CHANGE A SINGLE WORD.

DO NOT MODIFY PUNCTUATION.

DO NOT REWRITE.

DO NOT ADD OR REMOVE WORDS.

Break into separate lines exactly as follows:

The books are closed, the pages turned,
A journey you have bravely earned.

So much you've learned, so much you've sown,
Seeds of bright knowledge you have grown.

Your mind's a garden, rich and deep,
Where thoughts like flowers softly sleep,

And wake for you, so sharp and clear,
Dispelling every doubt and fear.

Remember all the hours you've spent,
Your dedication, heaven-sent.

That steady effort, day by day,
Will light your brilliant, chosen way.

This test is just a passing breeze,
A moment's pause among the trees.

The wisdom you now hold so true,
Will bloom forever within you.

So walk in with a steady stride,
Let courage be your faithful guide.

Believe in all the strength you bear,
You've got this, know that I care.

And when it's done, and you look back,
You'll see you stayed right on the track.

The future waits, a shining prize,
Reflected in your bright, clear eyes.

Go on and shine, you're ready now,
And take your well-deserved bow.

Animation Requirements:

* Each line appears individually.
* Use GSAP ScrollTrigger.
* Fade in + move upward.
* Lines appear sequentially as user scrolls.
* Lines remain visible after appearing.
* When scrolling back up:

  * reverse animation smoothly.
  * lines disappear in reverse order.
* Smooth easing.
* No flickering.
* Professional cinematic feel.
